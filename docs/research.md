Its lower entry bill weakens the expensive-software case. Neither candidate has a verified local customer invoice in this run. Our import is offline, using saved responses; no credentials are needed for the demo.

These establish the operating workflow, not proof that our base replicates the live service. The free base provides stored-evidence review and restore records; live collection and alert delivery are additional integration work.
